Where this all started
1966

Grocery didn’t inherit this fight. Grocery started it.

In 1966 a grocery store manager who couldn’t catch a shoplifter challenged his cousin to build something better. They called it “The Shoplifter’s Enemy.” It became Sensormatic — and the anti-theft tag on products everywhere.

He wasn’t a technologist. He was a grocery manager with a problem nobody else had solved yet.

Source: Sensormatic, 60th anniversary
